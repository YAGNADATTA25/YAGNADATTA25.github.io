MECHANICAL DESIGN / TEAM HYDRA

Design And Optimization of Push-to-Open Latch

From a cabinet-opening mechanism to a complete SolidWorks assembly: compliant design, material selection and additive-manufacturing considerations.

SolidWorksMechanical designAdditive manufacturingTopology optimization

The engineering problem

Design a push-to-open latch for kitchen cabinets that replaces a conventional handle and uses a compliant zigzag beam in place of a separate spring. The mechanism must guide the slider, store elastic energy during a push and release it to assist opening.

My contribution

I personally completed the entire latch design in SolidWorks within Team Hydra, including the housing, slider, zigzag beam and path-follower strip. The design connects component geometry, guided motion and a compliant return mechanism in one assembly.

PROJECT AT A GLANCE

CAD ownership within a team project

My role: complete SolidWorks design.

Team study: material selection, nonlinear beam analysis and component-level topology optimization.

Manufacturing approach: design for fused deposition modelling (FDM).

DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

Inside the mechanism.

Materials and manufacturing

The team report evaluates Nylon 6/10 for the flexible zigzag beam and polypropylene for the remaining latch components. The proposed FDM route informed the material and geometry discussion. Topology studies explored material removal while preserving functional regions such as the slider path and interfaces.
