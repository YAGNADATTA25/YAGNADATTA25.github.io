AI Driven Lifelong SLAM

Ongoing exploration of overhead RGB-D sensing and local occupancy maps for warehouse robots.

Infrastructure-assisted mapping with overhead RGB-D sensors. This ongoing project is documented in GitHub as Infrastructure-Assisted Lifelong SLAM.

SLAMRGB-DTF2

The question

Can overhead infrastructure sensing help mobile robots maintain useful maps in changing warehouse environments?

The approach

This ongoing work explores overhead RGB-D observations, point-cloud projection into local occupancy grids and spatial alignment through TF2. Navigation integration and long-term drift evaluation remain research goals.
