PROJECT 03 / MOBILE ROBOTICS

Vision-guided line tracking

Camera-based line detection and steering, with LiDAR-based reactive obstacle handling.

OpenCVPythonROS 2

The question

How can a mobile robot turn camera observations of a floor line into steering commands?

The approach

The documented pipeline uses OpenCV colour segmentation and centroid extraction to estimate line position. Image-space error drives angular steering, while LiDAR proximity readings trigger reactive obstacle handling.
